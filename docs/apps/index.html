Final Countdown

Serene interval routines & celebration countdowns

A mindful countdown and timer utility built for stretching routines, self-care intervals, and milestone celebration dates. Designed to run beautifully across iPhone, iPad, Apple Watch, and the Lock Screen with customizable chimes and zero distractions.

  • Dual Countdown Engine Flexible duration timers with periodic chime beeps alongside fixed-date arrival countdowns with celebratory confetti.
  • Dynamic Island & Widgets Interactive Live Activities with play/pause controls, plus home-screen and lock-screen accessory widgets for your soonest events.
  • Standalone Apple Watch App Extended runtime keep-awake sessions keep the screen visible during guided intervals without waking the watch mid-routine.
  • 100% On-Device & Private Timers and events are saved strictly to local SwiftData storage. Makes zero network calls and includes no analytics SDKs.

Point Taker

Courtside badminton scoreboard & rally counter

Turns your iPhone into a courtside umpire's scoreboard. Prop it up on the sideline, tap the side that won the rally, and let the app handle official BWF rally scoring, serve rotation, and deuce advantage rules so you can stay focused on the shuttle.

  • Official BWF Rally Logic Accurate 21-point game rules, deuce advantage tracking, and the official 30-point sudden-death cap with automated winner alerts.
  • Singles & Doubles Rotation Automatic serve switching and accurate odd/even service-court positioning with partner court swapping for doubles.
  • Arm's-Length Legibility High-contrast electric gold & pitch black palette with full-height tap panels designed for effortless glances from across the court.
  • Instant Undo & Match Restore Rolls back mis-taps with complete score and serve recovery. Matches save to local storage after every rally and restore seamlessly.
